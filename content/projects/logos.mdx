My logo design philosophy:

1. **Keep it simple.** The best logos gives viewers an immediate and clear sense of your product.
2. **Make it memorable.** Use simple geometry to create a novel aesthetic.
3. **Make it versatile.** It should look great in all contexts.

This is a collage of logos I designed for clients, personal projects, startups, and large businesses.
