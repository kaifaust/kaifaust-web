InfoPop augments your reality with meaningful data. We give our users the power to think, collaborate and be more efficient with mixed reality, spatial awareness, and third-party API integrations.

## About the Project

I was the startup founder of InfoPop, an iOS app launched on the App Store in September 2020. While it's not a functional app anymore, InfoPop represented an ambitious vision for how we interact with spatial information in our physical world.

## Core Concept

Search in 3D. Find anything. Scan and tag your physical world and share that information with others. Any space can be navigated with Augmented Reality.

InfoPop allowed users to:
- Scan and tag their physical environment
- Access spatial information overlaid on the real world
- Share tagged information with others
- Navigate spaces using AR technology

## App Details

- **Category:** Productivity
- **Price:** Free
- **Launched:** September 3, 2020
